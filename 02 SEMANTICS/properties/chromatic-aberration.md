# Chromatic Aberration

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-CHROMATIC-ABERRATION-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## 5W1H Orientation

**What:** A characteristic of optical imaging in which image formation varies with wavelength, producing wavelength-dependent geometric or focus-related deviation.

**Who/What can bear it:** Optical imaging systems, lenses, cameras, or defined optical configurations.

**Why:** To describe wavelength-dependent limitations in optical image formation.

## Semantic Definition

Chromatic aberration is a property of an optical imaging system describing wavelength-dependent departure in image formation, including differences in image position, focus, magnification, or related optical mapping behaviour.

## What This Property Characterizes

It characterizes wavelength-dependent optical behaviour. It does not itself denote a measured displacement, blur width, wavelength, or colour-fringing magnitude.

## Quantification Boundary

Chromatic-aberration measurements and parameters are quantitative realizations of this Property. Different measurement methods may characterize longitudinal, lateral, or other defined forms.

## Distinctions

Chromatic aberration is distinct from geometric distortion, which is not inherently wavelength-dependent, and from colour appearance or perceptual effects in a rendered representation.

## Evidence and Provenance

Supported by standardized optical and imaging terminology and measurement methods, including ISO 15795 and ISO 19084.

## Machine Interpretation

The Property may be attached to an eligible optical imaging bearer. Numerical aberration values require explicit quantitative and measurement context.

## Semantic Boundary

This page defines the characteristic concept. It does not canonicalize individual aberration coefficients, test procedures, lens specifications, or perceptual artefacts as the Property itself.
