# Spectral Response

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-SPECTRAL-RESPONSE-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## 5W1H Orientation

**What:** A characteristic describing how an imaging or sensing system responds as a function of wavelength.

**Who/What can bear it:** Sensors, cameras, spectral channels, or defined imaging configurations whose response varies with incident spectral content.

**Why:** To characterize wavelength-dependent sensing or imaging behaviour.

## Semantic Definition

Spectral response is a property describing the wavelength-dependent response behaviour of an imaging or sensing system under a defined configuration and response definition.

## What This Property Characterizes

It characterizes spectral dependence of system response. It does not by itself assert a particular response value, sensitivity coefficient, quantum efficiency, colour specification, or calibration state.

## Quantification Boundary

Response curves, wavelength-specific response values, spectral sensitivity values, or related measurements are quantitative realizations of this Property.

## Distinctions

Spectral response is distinct from quantum efficiency and responsivity, which may be quantitative concepts describing particular aspects of response. It is also distinct from spectral range, which concerns the domain over which a system is specified or responsive.

## Evidence and Provenance

Supported by imaging measurement practice, including EMVA 1288 characterization of wavelength-dependent sensor response.

## Machine Interpretation

A machine may attach the Property to an eligible sensing or imaging bearer and associate explicit spectral response data separately.

## Semantic Boundary

This page defines the characteristic abstraction only. It does not define a response curve, numerical coefficient, sensor specification, colour model, or vendor performance claim.
