# Optical Distortion

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-OPTICAL-DISTORTION-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## 5W1H Orientation

**What:** A characteristic describing geometric departure in the spatial mapping produced by an optical imaging system from the intended or reference mapping.

**Who/What can bear it:** Optical imaging systems, lenses, cameras, or defined imaging configurations, where the relevant optical mapping is attributable to that bearer.

**Where:** In the geometric imaging behaviour of the relevant optical path or imaging configuration.

**When:** Under a defined configuration and measurement or operating context.

**Why:** To characterize geometric image-forming behaviour and distinguish spatial mapping error from other image-quality characteristics.

**How:** Through a defined distortion model or measurement procedure that compares the observed mapping with a specified reference.

## Semantic Definition

Optical distortion is a property describing systematic geometric deviation in the spatial mapping produced by an optical imaging system relative to a defined ideal, reference, or calibration mapping.

## What This Property Characterizes

This Property characterizes geometric imaging behaviour. It concerns spatial mapping rather than sharpness, resolution, chromatic aberration, exposure, or perceptual appearance.

## Quantification Boundary

Measured distortion magnitudes, coefficients, percentages, displacement fields, or other derived parameters are quantitative realizations of this Property. They are not the Property itself.

## Distinctions

Optical distortion is distinct from chromatic aberration, which concerns wavelength-dependent image formation. It is also distinct from geometric resolution and from general image-quality judgments such as sharpness.

## Evidence and Provenance

The semantic decision is supported by imaging-standard terminology and measurement practice, including ISO 17850 and related optical-imaging measurement literature.

## Machine Interpretation

A machine may interpret this Property as a characteristic predicate applicable to an eligible imaging bearer. It MUST NOT infer a numerical distortion value without an explicit quantity or measurement result.

## Semantic Boundary

This document defines the characteristic concept only. It does not define a particular distortion coefficient, test chart, measurement procedure, lens specification, or vendor performance claim.
