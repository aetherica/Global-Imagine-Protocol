# Chromaticity

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-CHROMATICITY-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## Semantic Definition

Chromaticity is a property of a colour stimulus describing its chromatic aspect independently of its absolute luminous or radiant magnitude, under a defined colourimetric framework.

## Bearer / Applicability

Applicable to defined colour stimuli or colour-related representations when the relevant colourimetric framework and observer conditions are specified.

## Quantification Boundary

Chromaticity coordinates or other defined chromaticity descriptors are quantitative realizations of this Property. The coordinates are not themselves the Property concept.

## Distinctions

Chromaticity is distinct from colour appearance, which depends on viewing and perceptual conditions, and from a physical spectral distribution, which may produce a colour stimulus but is not identical to its chromaticity.

## Evidence and Provenance

Supported by CIE terminology, which defines chromaticity as a property of a colour stimulus expressed through defined chromaticity descriptors.

## Machine Interpretation

A machine may attach this Property only where the relevant colour stimulus and colourimetric framework are identified. A chromaticity coordinate MUST NOT be inferred without those definitions.

## Semantic Boundary

This page defines the characteristic concept. It does not define a particular colour space, coordinate pair, illuminant, observer function, or appearance judgment.
