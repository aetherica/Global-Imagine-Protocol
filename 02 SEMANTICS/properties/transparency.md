# Transparency

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-TRANSPARENCY-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## Semantic Definition

Transparency is a physical property describing the extent to which a material or medium permits relevant radiation to pass through it under a defined context.

## Bearer / Applicability

Primarily applicable to materials or media whose transmission of relevant radiation is being characterized.

## Quantification Boundary

Transmittance and related transmission ratios are quantitative realizations associated with the Property. They are not synonymous with the Property itself.

## Distinctions

Transparency is distinct from clarity, which concerns the visibility or preservation of distinguishable images through a transparent or semitransparent medium. Perceived transparency is a perceptual concept and must not be substituted for physical transparency.

## Evidence and Provenance

CIE terminology explicitly treats transparency as a physical property of a material or substance and distinguishes it from related optical and perceptual concepts.

## Machine Interpretation

A machine may attach transparency to an eligible material or medium. It MUST NOT infer a particular transmittance value without explicit quantitative information.

## Semantic Boundary

This page defines the physical characteristic concept only. Spectral, angular, wavelength-specific, or measured transmission quantities belong in the appropriate quantitative and contextual layers.
