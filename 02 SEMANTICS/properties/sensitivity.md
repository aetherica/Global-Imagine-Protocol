# Sensitivity

**ID:** SEM-PROPERTY-SENSITIVITY-001
**Status:** CANONICAL
**Semantic Type:** PROPERTY

## 5W1H Orientation

**What:** A characteristic describing how a defined system's response changes in relation to a defined input.

**Who/What can bear it:** Measuring systems, sensors, imaging systems, or other defined systems for which sensitivity is meaningfully specified.

**Why:** To characterize input-response behaviour independently of any single measured value.

## Semantic Definition

Sensitivity is a property describing the response dependence of a defined system on changes in a defined input under a specified measurement or operating context.

## What This Property Characterizes

This Property characterizes input-response behaviour. Its exact interpretation depends on the defined system, input, output, and context.

## Quantification Boundary

Sensitivity coefficients, slopes, thresholds, or other numerical sensitivity measures are quantitative realizations. They require an explicit measurement model, input/output definition, and relevant conditions.

## Distinctions

Sensitivity is not synonymous with exposure, gain, responsivity, quantum efficiency, or detector noise. Those concepts may characterize related but distinct aspects of a system.

## Evidence and Provenance

The Property classification is supported by measurement science terminology, including VIM treatment of sensitivity as a property of a measuring system, together with imaging characterization practice.

## Machine Interpretation

Machines MUST NOT infer a specific sensitivity value from the presence of this Property alone.

## Semantic Boundary

This page defines the general characteristic concept. Domain-specific numerical definitions and sensitivity coefficients belong in the appropriate quantitative and measurement layers.
