## Research Basis

Research compared ISO/IEC AI terminology, ISO/IEC ML framework terminology, NIST AI model terminology, OMG modelling and MDA traditions, W3C provenance and ML-schema work, and scientific-model / scientific-representation literature. The sources converge on the usefulness of distinguishing a model as a purpose-specific construct from the method, implementation, provenance, and carrier used around it, while differing substantially on model types and the exact nature of representation.
