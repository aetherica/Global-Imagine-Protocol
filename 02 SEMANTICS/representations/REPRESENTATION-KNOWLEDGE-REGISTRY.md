# Representation Knowledge Registry

**Status:** Active Semantic Knowledge Registry  
**Version:** 2.0.0  
**Purpose:** Retain representation-related concepts, decisions, evidence status, and routing without becoming a parallel canonical knowledge base.

## Registry Rules

The registry follows the GIOP Knowledge Entry & Canonicalization Rule:

`RETAIN → CLASSIFY → VERIFY → CONFLICT ANALYSIS → SYNTHESIZE → DECIDE → AUTHOR → VALIDATE`

A registry record is not automatically canonical. Canonical semantic content is authoritative only in the appropriate semantic entry.

## Cross-Layer Controls

- State remains distinct from State Representation.
- Belief Representation remains distinct from belief/state semantics.
- Object- and Relation-Centric Representation do not replace Class, Property, State, Relation, or Perception semantics.
- Affordance and Action Representation do not replace Action execution, Activity, Capability, or Procedure.
- Event and Trajectory Representation do not replace Event/Process/State semantics.
- Scene, World, Map, Spatiotemporal, and Neural Field representations remain representation forms rather than automatic Models or Processes.
- Causal and Predictive Representation do not automatically become Causal/Predictive Models.
- Program and Intermediate Representation do not replace Program execution or Algorithm semantics.
- Digital-Twin Representation does not replace the physical twin or complete digital-twin system.
- Skill Representation does not replace skill/capability or a policy/model.
- Dataset, File, Bitstream, Result, Perception, Measurement, and Context remain distinct.
