## Purpose

Record the repository-level closure and final promotion audit after the Representation semantic area was expanded from the generic batch through the first controlled advanced extension dataset.

## Audit Gates

### Identity

Passed. Stable semantic IDs, preferred names, and dedicated canonical entries are present.

### Primary Responsibility

Passed. Each promoted concept has a distinct representation responsibility. Closely related concepts are separated by organizing principle or semantic role rather than lexical difference.

### Existing-Entry Check

Passed. Existing Representation, Relation, State, Activity, Process, Model, Class, Property, Perception, Quantity, Context, and other authorities were preserved. No promoted entry claims ownership of another semantic layer merely because it may encode information about it.

### Boundary

Passed. Particular care was applied to:

- Object-Centric vs Relation-Centric vs Scene Representation;
- Affordance vs Action vs Skill Representation;
- Event vs State vs Activity/Process;
- Spatiotemporal vs Trajectory Representation;
- Causal/Predictive Representation vs Causal/Predictive Model;
- Neural Field vs broad Neural Representation;
- Program vs Intermediate Representation;
- Digital-Twin Representation vs Digital-Twin System/Model;
- World vs Scene vs Map Representation.

### Provenance / Evidence

Passed. Evidence was drawn across multiple source traditions rather than constrained to a fixed source family, including standards, graph/data systems, robotics, computer vision, event-based sensing, causal learning, world models, neural fields, programming languages, digital twins, and mapping.

External sources remain evidence for GIOP semantic synthesis and are not copied as GIOP authority.

### Relation Authority

Passed. Existing admitted GIOP relation vocabulary is used. No new relation authority was silently introduced.

### Visitor Universe

Passed. Visitor Universe remains retrieval and entry-depth routing only. No audience-specific semantic copies were created.

### Retrieval

Passed. All promoted entries expose deterministic identity, semantic responsibility, distinctions/boundaries, validation context, lifecycle, and retrieval anchors.

### Lifecycle

Passed. The promoted concepts are Active canonical at their scoped semantic responsibility. Narrower implementations, codecs, model families, datasets, and realization variants remain separately governable.

### Cross-Layer Validation

Passed. The promotion does not replace State, Relation, Property, Quantity, Activity, Process, Context, Condition, Perception, Model, Algorithm, Software, Dataset, File, Bitstream, Result, or other established authorities.

## Integrity / Branch Boundary

All writes in this completion pass targeted `v3.1-tree-architecture`. `main` was not targeted.

The immutable proof ledger was not modified because no explicit immutable proof-ledger release operation was requested.

## Closure Decision

The Representation folder is closed for the first advanced extension batch. The generic nucleus and all 18 advanced promoted concepts are now canonically integrated.

Future Representation concepts must enter through a new independently scoped intake and cannot be promoted by lexical similarity or association with this closed batch.
