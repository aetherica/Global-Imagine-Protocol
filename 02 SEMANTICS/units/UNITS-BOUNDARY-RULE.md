# Units Boundary Rule

## Ownership

Units owns measurement-unit identity and the organization of units within systems of units.

## Mandatory non-collapse rules

- Unit ≠ Quantity
- Unit ≠ Quantity Value
- Unit ≠ Quantity Dimension
- Unit ≠ Measurement
- Unit ≠ Measurement Result
- Unit ≠ Measuring Instrument
- Unit ≠ Measurement Procedure
- Unit ≠ Unit Symbol
- Unit ≠ Unit Code
- Unit ≠ Prefix
- Unit ≠ Conversion Relationship
- Unit ≠ Conversion Factor
- Unit ≠ System of Quantities

## Quantity boundary

Quantity answers what measurable aspect is being quantified. Unit supplies the reference used to express its value. Quantity Dimension remains under Quantity ownership.

## Measurement boundary

Measurement is an activity/process of obtaining or establishing quantity information. Unit is the reference structure used in expressing the resulting value.

## Representation boundary

Names, symbols, codes and serialized expressions are representational or identifying forms. They must not create duplicate Unit identities.

## Composition boundary

Compound expressions are structural constructions from units. They do not require one independent canonical semantic page per possible expression.

## Conversion boundary

Conversion is a relationship or operation between unit representations/values, not a replacement for either unit's semantic identity.

## Domain admission rule

A domain term is admitted as a Unit only when evidence establishes that it functions as a governed measurement unit. Numerical scales, counts, structural features, labels, parameters, or reference values do not qualify merely because they carry numbers.

## System boundary

System of Units is a distinct semantic concept. Base/derived/coherent status must be interpreted relative to the applicable system; no universal system-independent coherence claim is permitted.
