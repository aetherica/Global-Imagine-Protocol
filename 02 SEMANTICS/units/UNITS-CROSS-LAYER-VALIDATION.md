# Units Cross-Layer Validation

## Quantity

PASS target: Units does not redefine Quantity or Quantity Dimension. Quantity remains the owner of measurable aspects and quantity-dimension semantics.

## Measurement and Results

PASS target: Units does not absorb Measurement, Measurement Procedure, or Measurement Result. Unit is a reference used in expressing quantity values.

## Representation

PASS target: Unit name, symbol, code, and expression are not alternate Unit identities.

## Relations

PASS target: no new relation predicate is introduced. Existing GIOP relation authority remains unchanged.

## Properties / States / Conditions

PASS target: numerical or measured characteristics do not become Units merely because they are expressed with numbers.

## System semantics

PASS target: System of Units is distinct from Unit and System of Quantities. Coherence is interpreted relative to the specified system.

## Domain extensions

PASS target: information, logarithmic, historical, specialized, and domain-specific units remain controlled/deferred until independent evidence and boundary review.

## Visitor Universe

PASS target: visitor categories alter orientation and retrieval only; they do not create alternate Unit identities.

## Registry

PASS target: semantic IDs remain semantic-layer identities; registry authority is not imported into the semantic definition.

## Gate status

Validation target prepared for implementation-complete candidate closure. Canonical promotion remains a separate Gate-J decision.
