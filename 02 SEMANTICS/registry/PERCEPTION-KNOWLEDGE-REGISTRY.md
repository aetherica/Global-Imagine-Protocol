## Registry Purpose

This registry retains recovered claims, evidence status, candidate concepts, canonicalization decisions, boundaries, provenance, and reconsideration triggers for the Perception layer.

It is a retention and decision/audit layer, not a parallel ontology or second canonical knowledge base.

## Evidence and Epistemic Controls

Research evidence may establish terminology, phenomena, methodological support, or candidate responsibility. It does not automatically create a GIOP canonical identity.

Claims must remain distinguishable as source statements, evidence, inference, GIOP synthesis, or canonical decision.

## Visitor Universe Rule

Visitor Universe categories do not create registry records or semantic variants. They influence access depth and retrieval projections only.

## Reconsideration Triggers

Reopen a candidate when:

- an authoritative definition establishes independent semantic responsibility;
- interoperability requires a stable identity;
- the candidate cannot be represented without semantic loss under generic Perception;
- a neighbouring layer claims the same responsibility;
- new evidence materially changes the scope or boundary;
- a future domain implementation requires a formally governed specialization.
