## 5W1H Orientation

### What
Textile is a domain concept covering flexible textile materials, structures, and articles formed from fibres, filaments, yarns, or related constituents.

### Why
A domain-level textile concept is needed to organize fibre, yarn, fabric, construction, finish, and article semantics without replacing generic Material.

### Who
Relevant to textile science, apparel, manufacturing, design, imaging, e-commerce, quality control, and AI systems.

### Where
Fibres, yarns, fabrics, finished textile articles, garments, technical textiles, and virtual/digital representations.

### When
Textile identity and properties may change through manufacturing, finishing, wear, laundering, damage, or transformation.

### How
Textiles are characterized by constituent materials, structural organization, surface/finish, intended use, and observable or measured properties.

## Semantic Definition
**Textile** is a domain-qualified material or article concept involving textile fibres, yarns, fabrics, or related flexible textile structures.

## Scope / Boundary
Textile does not replace generic Material. Garments are assembled wearable articles and belong to the Garment domain, although a garment may contain textile materials.

## Core Distinctions
Textile vs Fibre: domain/article level vs constituent level.

Textile vs Fabric: textile is broader; fabric is a specific constructed textile structure.

Textile vs Garment: material/structure vs assembled wearable article.
