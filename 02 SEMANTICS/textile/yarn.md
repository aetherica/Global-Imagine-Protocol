## 5W1H Orientation

### What
Yarn is a continuous or assembled textile strand constructed from fibres, filaments, or related textile elements for textile formation or use.

### Why
Yarn is a principal intermediate structure between fibre-level constituents and many fabric constructions.

### Who
Textile manufacturers, designers, engineers, analysts, and machine-vision systems use yarn semantics.

### Where
Spinning, knitting, weaving, braiding, embroidery, composites, and finished textile structures.

### When
Yarn characteristics may change through spinning, plying, cabling, finishing, tension, moisture, wear, or damage.

### How
Yarn is described by constituent type, morphology, construction, linear density, twist, ply/cable structure, and other properties.

## Semantic Definition
**Yarn** is a textile strand structure assembled from fibres, filaments, or combinations thereof and intended for textile construction or related textile use.

## Scope / Boundary
Yarn construction processes are Processes/Activities; measurements such as linear density or twist are Quantities; physical characteristics remain Properties.

## Core Distinctions
Yarn vs Fibre: assembled strand vs constituent.

Yarn vs Fabric: strand structure vs higher-order textile structure.

Single yarn vs plied/cabled yarn: construction distinctions, not separate generic semantic layers.
