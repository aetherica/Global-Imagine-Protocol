## Semantic Decision

Physical Attachment is retained as `SEM-ATTACHMENT-CANDIDATE-001`, a bounded Relation-layer candidate pattern for physical/configurational attachment. The candidate does not create a new ontology layer and does not modify the existing Relation predicate set.

The bare lexical form `Attachment` is retained only as a retrieval/alias form. The preferred semantic identity is `Physical Attachment` to reduce collision with digital, psychological, anatomical-site, and device meanings.

## Residual / Deferred Scope

Strong/weak attachment, mounting, binding, adhesion, joining, joints, attachment points/sites, and domain-specific fastening concepts remain controlled vocabulary or domain candidates. Each requires independent semantic responsibility analysis before any promotion.

Relation logical characteristics remain unresolved until an explicit Relation-layer governance pass. External ontology axioms are not imported automatically.

## Closure Determination

The folder is implementation-complete for the bounded candidate scope after final research refinement. This closure does **not** mean canonical promotion. Promotion remains subject to a future independent Relation-layer review and explicit governance approval.
