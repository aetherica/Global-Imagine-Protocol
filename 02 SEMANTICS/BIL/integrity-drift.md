# BIL Integrity Drift

**Semantic ID:** `SEM-BIL-INTEGRITY-DRIFT-001`  
**Preferred Name:** Integrity Drift  
**Semantic Class:** BIL Control Concept  
**Domain:** Biological / Configurational Integrity  
**Status:** Candidate

> **Integrity Drift** is a change in an integrity-relevant attribute, relation, configuration, or correspondence over a defined temporal or version interval that may accumulate or become materially significant.

## 5W1H Orientation

### What
A change pattern assessed across time, versions, frames or repeated transformations.

### Why
Small changes can accumulate into identity, structure, appearance, configuration or temporal inconsistency even when no single observation is decisive.

### Who
Relevant to video specialists, VFX teams, validation engineers, digital-human systems, wildlife re-identification researchers and auditors.

### Where
Video, animation, multi-version assets, iterative editing, generated-media sequences, 3D/4D and lifecycle comparisons.

### When
Requires a defined comparison interval or version relationship.

### How
`Reference/Previous State → Comparison Interval → Observed Change Pattern → Integrity Interpretation`.

## Semantic Definition

**Integrity Drift** is the BIL control concept denoting an accumulating or materially relevant change in an integrity-sensitive target across a defined comparison interval.

## Core Distinctions

Drift ≠ any change; ≠ permitted variation; ≠ isolated deviation; ≠ error; ≠ proof of manipulation.

## Scope and Boundary

Drift may concern identity, geometry, markings, appearance, interaction, configuration, trajectory, temporal correspondence or representation consistency. Temporal semantics remain owned by `temporal/`.

## Cross-Domain Significance

Drift may be measured or observed through Activity, Result, Representation and Temporal layers. BIL determines whether the change is integrity-relevant under the profile and claim.

## Trust and Evidence

Drift assessments require comparison basis, interval/version, observability, uncertainty and provenance. Accumulated inferred changes must remain distinguishable from directly observed changes.

## Visitor Universe

D0 explains gradual change; D1 distinguishes drift/deviation/variation; D2 gives video/profile cases; D3 exposes temporal metrics and uncertainty; D4 exposes trajectories/version graphs.
