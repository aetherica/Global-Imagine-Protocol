# BIL Integrity Drift

Status: CANDIDATE — BIL V3.1
Semantic ID: SEM-BIL-INTEGRITY-DRIFT-001

## Definition
Integrity Drift is a change in an integrity-relevant attribute, relation, configuration, or correspondence over a defined comparison interval that may accumulate or become materially significant.

## Boundary
Drift ≠ any change; Drift ≠ permitted variation; Drift ≠ isolated deviation; Drift ≠ proof of manipulation.

## Requirements
Drift requires a comparison basis, temporal or version interval, scope, direction where meaningful, and uncertainty/observability context.

## Existing routing
Temporal aspects route to Temporal; observed changes to Activity/Result/evidence; BIL interprets integrity significance.
