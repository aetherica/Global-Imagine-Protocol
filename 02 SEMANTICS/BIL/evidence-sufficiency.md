# BIL Evidence Sufficiency

Status: CANDIDATE — BIL V3.1
Semantic ID: SEM-BIL-EVIDENCE-SUFFICIENCY-001

## Definition
Evidence Sufficiency is the scoped determination of whether available evidence is adequate for a particular Integrity Claim at a declared decision threshold.

## Boundary
Sufficiency ≠ confidence; Sufficiency ≠ truth; Sufficiency ≠ completeness of the source; Sufficiency ≠ a universal threshold.

## Factors
Claim scope, reference quality, observability, evidence independence, measurement uncertainty, occlusion, representation limits, temporal coverage, provenance and conflict.

## Rule
Insufficient evidence must remain distinct from inconsistency or failure.

## Visitor Universe
Simple visitors see sufficient/insufficient rationale; technical visitors receive evidence coverage and threshold logic.
