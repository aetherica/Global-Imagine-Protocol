# BIL Integrity Decision

Status: CANDIDATE — BIL V3.1
Semantic ID: SEM-BIL-INTEGRITY-DECISION-001

## Definition
An Integrity Decision is the qualified outcome assigned to an Integrity Evaluation within a declared scope, evidence state and decision policy.

## Candidate outcome vocabulary
Consistent; consistent with permitted variation; declared transformation; insufficient evidence; uncertain; inconsistent; conflicting evidence; potential integrity threat; requires adjudication.

## Boundary
Decision ≠ truth; Decision ≠ evidence; Decision ≠ Result; Decision ≠ binary pass/fail unless a profile explicitly defines such a policy.

## Rule
Every decision carries scope, policy version, evidence status, provenance and lifecycle state.
