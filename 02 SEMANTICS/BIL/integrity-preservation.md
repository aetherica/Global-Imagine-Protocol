# BIL Preservation

Status: CANDIDATE — BIL V3.1
Semantic ID: SEM-BIL-PRESERVATION-001

## Definition
Integrity Preservation is the maintained correspondence of specified integrity-relevant characteristics or configurations across a declared transformation, representation, or lifecycle interval.

## Boundary
Preservation ≠ immutability; Preservation ≠ identity; Preservation ≠ exact pixel equality; Preservation ≠ absence of all change.

## Scope
Preservation may be identity-, structure-, interaction-, appearance-, spatial-, temporal-, or context-specific.

## Rule
A preservation claim must declare what is preserved, relative to which reference, across which transformation/context, and with what permitted variation.
