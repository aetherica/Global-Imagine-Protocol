# BIL Integrity Preservation

**Semantic ID:** `SEM-BIL-PRESERVATION-001`  
**Preferred Name:** Integrity Preservation  
**Semantic Class:** BIL Control Concept  
**Domain:** Biological / Configurational Integrity  
**Status:** Candidate  
**Version:** 0.2.0

> **Integrity Preservation** is the maintained correspondence of specified integrity-relevant characteristics or configurations across a declared transformation, representation, version, or lifecycle interval.

## 5W1H Orientation

### What
A claim-oriented concept describing what remains consistent while other aspects may change.

### Why
Transformation is not identical to failure. BIL needs a way to express preservation of selected invariants without requiring pixel-level or total immutability.

### Who
Relevant to photographers, filmmakers, VFX teams, digital-human systems, imaging scientists, validation engineers and researchers.

### Where
Still-to-still, image-to-image, image-to-video, video-to-video, VFX, 3D/4D, multi-view and iterative editing contexts.

### When
Across a declared transformation or comparison interval.

### How
`Reference → Declared Transformation → Preserved Integrity Dimensions + Permitted Variation → Validation → Decision`.

## Semantic Definition

**Integrity Preservation** is the BIL control concept denoting continued correspondence of specified integrity-relevant information across a declared change context.

## Core Distinctions

Preservation ≠ immutability; ≠ identity; ≠ exact pixel equality; ≠ absence of all change; ≠ guarantee of physical plausibility.

## Scope and Boundary

Preservation may be identity-, structural-, interaction-, appearance-, spatial-, temporal-, species-, contextual- or representation-specific. The claim must state what is preserved.

## Cross-Domain Significance

Preservation interprets changes originating in Process, Activity, Representation, State, Relation and Temporal layers. It does not replace those semantics.

## Trust and Evidence

A preservation claim requires a declared reference, transformation context, permitted variation, evidence, validation state and provenance.
