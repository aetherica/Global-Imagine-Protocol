# BIL Integrity Adjudication

Status: CANDIDATE — BIL V3.1
Semantic ID: SEM-BIL-INTEGRITY-ADJUDICATION-001

## Definition
Integrity Adjudication is a controlled resolution activity for cases where evidence, constraints, reference scope, or automated evaluations conflict or remain materially ambiguous.

## Boundary
Adjudication ≠ ordinary validation; Adjudication ≠ confidence score; Adjudication ≠ manual override without provenance.

## Requirements
Record disputed claims, evidence sources, conflict type, decision authority, rationale, policy version, final disposition and audit trail.

## Rule
An unresolved conflict remains unresolved until adjudication criteria are satisfied.
