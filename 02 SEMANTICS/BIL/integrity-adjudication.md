# BIL Integrity Adjudication

**Semantic ID:** `SEM-BIL-INTEGRITY-ADJUDICATION-001`  
**Preferred Name:** Integrity Adjudication  
**Semantic Class:** BIL Control Concept  
**Domain:** Biological / Configurational Integrity  
**Status:** Candidate  
**Version:** 0.2.0

> **Integrity Adjudication** is the controlled resolution activity for material conflict or ambiguity among evidence, constraints, reference scope, automated evaluations, or decision policies.

## 5W1H Orientation

### What
A formal resolution process for cases that cannot be safely resolved by ordinary validation alone.

### Why
Conflicting evidence, insufficient observability, policy conflict, identity ambiguity, or competing expert interpretations may require explicit adjudication.

### Who
Expert reviewers, validation authorities, auditors, scientific reviewers and designated decision owners.

### Where
High-stakes, ambiguous, conflicted, safety-sensitive, multi-source or manually reviewed BIL cases.

### When
After or during evaluation when material conflict remains unresolved.

### How
Record the disputed claim, evidence, conflict type, authority, rationale, policy/version and final disposition.

## Semantic Definition

**Integrity Adjudication** is the BIL control concept denoting the governed resolution of material integrity conflicts or ambiguities that remain after ordinary evaluation.

## Core Distinctions

Adjudication ≠ ordinary validation; ≠ confidence score; ≠ arbitrary manual override; ≠ evidence deletion; ≠ provenance replacement.

## Scope and Boundary

An unresolved conflict remains unresolved until applicable adjudication criteria are satisfied. The process must preserve alternative evidence and the rationale for the final disposition.

## Cross-Domain Significance

Adjudication may consume evidence from Representation, Quantity, Activity, Result, Relation, Provenance and external domain expertise. The underlying semantics remain owned by those layers.

## Trust and Evidence

Decision authority, reviewer identity where applicable, rationale, evidence versions, policy version and audit trail are mandatory for a governed adjudication record.
