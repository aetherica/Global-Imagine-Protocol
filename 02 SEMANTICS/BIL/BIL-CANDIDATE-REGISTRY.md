## Non-native routed topics
Human, animal, plant, anatomy, species, phone, bag, fabric, water, surface, sports, dance, wildlife, VFX, optics, metrics, algorithms, software and provenance mechanisms remain owned by their existing domain/layer unless a future validation proves a BIL-specific concept is required.

## Promotion rule
Registry membership is not semantic authority. Promotion requires identity, primary responsibility, non-equivalence, cross-layer validation, provenance, Visitor Universe validation, retrieval integrity, lifecycle and Gate-J approval.
