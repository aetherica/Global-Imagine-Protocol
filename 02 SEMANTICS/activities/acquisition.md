## 5W1H Orientation
### What
Acquisition is an Activity in which information or data is obtained or captured from a defined source, scene, object, phenomenon, or operational context.
### Why
It represents the actual capture/obtaining event and separates that event from Camera, Sensor, Lens, Measuring System, image/data product, and acquisition Procedure.
### Who
A Camera, Sensor, Measuring System, computational system, human-operated setup, or coordinated system may perform or enable it.
### Where
Photography, imaging science, machine vision, remote sensing, laboratory measurement, scientific instrumentation, simulation, virtual production, or other capture contexts.
### When
Capture time, exposure interval, phenomenon time, trigger time, and result availability may be distinct.
### How
Acquisition uses an acquisition system or participating entities under relevant Conditions and according to a Procedure or operational configuration where applicable.

## Semantic Definition
**Acquisition** is an Activity in which information or data is obtained or captured from a defined source, scene, object, phenomenon, or operational context. It does not denote the instrument, optical component, sensor, or resulting image/data entity.

## Semantic Responsibility
Acquisition owns the capture/obtaining occurrence in an information acquisition chain.

## Core Distinctions
`Acquisition ≠ Camera`: Camera is a system-level capture entity.  
`Acquisition ≠ Sensor`: Sensor is a sensing entity/subsystem.  
`Acquisition ≠ Lens`: Lens is an optical component/assembly.  
`Acquisition ≠ Image/Data`: image or dataset is an entity/representation generated by acquisition.  
`Acquisition ≠ Observation`: acquisition emphasizes capture; observation emphasizes obtaining information about a phenomenon/property.  
`Acquisition ≠ Measurement`: capture does not automatically imply quantitative measurement.

## Optical Acquisition Context
```text
Camera → Lens → Sensor → Acquisition Activity → Acquired Data / Image / Representation
```
This is a conceptual dependency, not a mandatory physical hierarchy.

## Cross-Domain Significance
Acquisition is the operational capture concept connecting physical or simulated source context with information products while preserving the distinction between system architecture and occurrence.
