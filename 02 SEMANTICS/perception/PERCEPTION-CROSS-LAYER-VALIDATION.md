## Perceptual Attribute Validation

Perceptual attributes such as brightness, lightness, hue, colourfulness, and chroma may be described or quantified. Quantification does not transfer their primary responsibility to Quantity.

Example routing:

`Luminance → Quantity`  
`Brightness → Perceptual Attribute`  
`Brightness Rating → Response / Value / Result`

## Psychophysical Validation

The pattern:

`Stimulus → Procedure / Task → Observer → Response → Measurement / Analysis → Perceptual Claim`

is an evidence pathway. It must not be interpreted as a universal ontology pipeline or as an identity chain in which response or measurement becomes Perception.

## Cognition Validation

Perception and cognition can be strongly coupled. Recognition, categorization, understanding, reasoning, decision, and action remain distinct responsibilities unless future analysis demonstrates otherwise for a specific concept.

## Visitor Universe Validation

No visitor-specific semantic variants are permitted. Visitor Universe may determine retrieval depth or explanatory orientation only.

## Validation Outcome

The generic Perception concept is sufficiently distinct to serve as a canonical semantic anchor. Research-backed specialization families are retained as controlled candidates. No specialized concept is promoted solely by frequency of terminology use.
