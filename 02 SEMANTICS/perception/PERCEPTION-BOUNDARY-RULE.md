# Perception Boundary Rule

**Semantic Layer:** Perception  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Status:** Active

## Governing Rule

The Perception layer owns reusable semantics whose primary responsibility is perceptual experience or interpretation. It does not own the observer entity, the physical stimulus, the observation activity, the model used to predict response, or the numerical result used to report evidence.

## Hard Boundaries

- Perception ≠ Observer.
- Perception ≠ Observer Model.
- Perception ≠ Observation.
- Perception ≠ Property.
- Perception ≠ Quantity or Quantity Value.
- Perception ≠ State, Condition, or Context.
- Perception ≠ Representation or Dataset.
- Perceptual report is evidence/representation of perception, not necessarily the perception itself.
- A psychophysical metric is not automatically the perceptual phenomenon it evaluates.
- A computational prediction is not automatically a perceptual experience.

## Psychophysics Boundary

Psychophysical measurement links controlled stimuli to observer responses. The measured response, task performance, threshold, rating, or derived metric may be represented as a Quantity, Value, Activity result, or other appropriate semantic layer. The perceptual phenomenon remains distinct.

## Colorimetric Boundary

CIE standard observers and colour-matching functions are observer/model semantics and representations. They can support colorimetric computations and perceptual research but must not be conflated with generic Perception.

## Visitor Neutrality

Visitor Universe categories influence access depth and retrieval only. They never determine the semantic identity of a perceptual concept.
