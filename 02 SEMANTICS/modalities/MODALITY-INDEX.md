## Routed / Excluded Concepts

- `Representation`, `Format`, `Encoding`, and `Media Type` remain under Representation semantics.
- `Activity`, `Observation`, `Acquisition`, `Processing`, and `Calibration` remain under Activities.
- `Process` remains under Processes.
- `Perception` remains under Perception.
- `Multimodal` is not a single modality subtype.

## Entry-Depth Map

General visitors → definition and basic examples.  
Learners/educators → distinctions and structure.  
Creative/imaging/science visitors → domain interpretation and applied boundaries.  
Engineering/AI/system visitors → cross-layer and machine semantics.  
Standards/QA/preservation/machine consumers → IDs, status, evidence, provenance, validation, and controlled terminology.

Entry depth changes access and presentation, not canonical identity.
