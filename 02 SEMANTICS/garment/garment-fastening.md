## 5W1H Orientation
Garment Fastening is a garment-domain mechanism or component used to secure, close, adjust, or retain parts of a garment.

## Semantic Definition
**Garment Fastening** is a mechanism, component, or configured closure used to secure or adjust a garment or its components during intended use.

## Scope / Boundary
Includes buttons, zippers, snaps, hooks, ties, hook-and-loop and equivalent closures when considered as garment-domain mechanisms. Generic fastener/material semantics remain external. Fastening operations belong to Activity/Process.

## Core Distinctions
**Fastening vs Component:** fastening is a functional/structural role; a component may or may not serve that role.  
**Fastening vs Closure State:** mechanism versus current open/closed condition.  
**Fastening vs Material:** mechanism/role versus constituent substance.

## Cross-Domain Significance
Connects Garment Component, Textile, Material, State, Activity, Process, and Representation.

## Trust / Evidence
Fastening type should be distinguished from inferred hidden closure structure in imagery or reconstruction.

## Visitor Universe
Novice: common closure mechanisms. Expert: construction role, adjustment, hidden mechanisms, and state. Machine: fastening type, component relation, state, evidence.
