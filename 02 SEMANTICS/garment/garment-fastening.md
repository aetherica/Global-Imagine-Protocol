## 5W1H Orientation

### What
Garment Fastening is the mechanism or configured means by which garment portions are joined, closed, secured, or adjusted for wearing, opening, removal, or fit.

### Why
Fastening semantics describe functional closure and adjustment without confusing the fastening mechanism with the garment component that carries it or the operation used to engage it.

### Who
Designers, garment technologists, manufacturers, users, inspectors, repair specialists, and digital garment systems use fastening semantics.

### Where
Fastenings occur at openings, joins, adjustable regions, and other locations where garment components must be secured or released.

### When
A fastening has states such as engaged, disengaged, adjusted, damaged, or replaced. The current state belongs to State semantics where applicable.

### How
Fastening is achieved by mechanisms such as buttons, zippers, hooks, ties, snaps, closures, or other designed means. The specific mechanism is a domain specialization, while generic actions remain Activity/Process semantics.

## Semantic Definition
Garment Fastening is a garment-specific securing or closure mechanism that controls the connection or adjustment of garment components during use.

## Scope / Boundary
Fastening owns the garment-domain concept. Construction owns its integration into the garment; Activity/Process owns fastening/unfastening operations; State owns current engagement state; Relation expresses generic connections.

## Core Distinctions
- Fastening ≠ fastening action.
- Fastening ≠ garment component in general.
- Fastening ≠ construction as a whole.
- Fastening mechanism ≠ current fastening state.

## Cross-Domain Significance
Fastening influences fit, accessibility, appearance, safety, usability, repair, and interaction with the wearer. It can also be visually significant in garment representations.

## Trust / Evidence
Evidence may include physical inspection, construction records, product documentation, images, or digital models. Hidden closure mechanisms require appropriate evidence qualification.

## Visitor Universe
Novices need mechanism-versus-action distinction. Experts need closure types, engagement states, adjustment, placement, failure, and repair. Machines need fastening type, component relations, and state.

## Lifecycle
Design → integration → use/engagement → wear or failure → repair/replacement → retirement.
