## 5W1H Orientation
A Garment Component is a constituent part of a garment that participates in its construction, function, coverage, support, or closure.

## Semantic Definition
**Garment Component** is a constituent element of an assembled garment with a defined structural, functional, or constructional role.

## Scope / Boundary
Includes domain-recognizable components such as sleeves, collars, cuffs, waistbands, pockets, panels, linings, and closure components. Generic Part/Relation semantics remain in their existing layers; textile constituents remain Textile-owned.

## Core Distinctions
**Component vs Garment:** part versus assembled article.  
**Component vs Accessory:** a component participates in garment construction; an accessory may accompany or attach without being structurally constitutive.  
**Component vs Material:** component is an article part; material describes what it is made from.

## Cross-Domain Significance
Connects Garment with Part-of relations, Textile, Material, Representation, Quantity, State, and BIL integrity evaluation.

## Trust / Evidence
Component claims should distinguish observed structure from inferred construction, particularly in imagery or reconstructed 3D garments.

## Visitor Universe
Novice: garment versus component. Expert: component identity, configuration, construction, and cross-representation consistency. Machine: stable component ID, part relations, evidence, and uncertainty.
