# GIOP V3.1 — Interoperability Policy

Status: CANONICAL

## Purpose

Ensure GIOP can relate knowledge across domains, vendors, implementations, standards, and external systems without losing identity or meaning.

## Rules

External systems may be mapped to GIOP without being renamed as if they were native GIOP concepts.

Mappings must preserve source terminology, target identity, direction, scope, and transformation assumptions.

Interoperability claims must distinguish exact equivalence, compatible interpretation, partial mapping, approximation, and unresolved relation.

Cross-domain links should resolve through stable semantic or registry identity rather than fragile textual coincidence.
