# GIOP V3.1 — Scope

Status: CANONICAL

## In scope

GIOP covers the knowledge infrastructure required to describe imaging from physical observation and capture through optics, sensors, representations, computation, AI, vision, production, delivery, display, research, standards, engineering, runtime, commercial use, trust, and historical context.

## Foundation-specific scope

FOUNDATION covers the framework governing that knowledge: mission, vision, principles, identity, namespace, documentation, terminology policy, governance, change, compatibility, interoperability, quality, release, contribution, roadmap, and preservation boundaries.

## Out of scope for Foundation

Canonical domain definitions, canonical registered entities, vendor-specific product truth, implementation code as semantic authority, and generated presentation outputs.

Those responsibilities belong to their appropriate downstream roots.
