# GIOP V3.1 — Mission

Status: CANONICAL

GIOP exists to make imaging knowledge coherent enough to be understood by people, precise enough to be used by professionals, structured enough to be processed by computers, and explicit enough to be useful to AI systems.

The mission is to build a durable, auditable knowledge infrastructure spanning the science, technology, craft, computation, and evolving intelligence of imaging.

The mission is achieved by maintaining clear responsibility boundaries, explicit identity, controlled language, traceable evidence, reproducible technical definitions, and preserved historical context.

GIOP is a knowledge system, not merely a collection of documents.
