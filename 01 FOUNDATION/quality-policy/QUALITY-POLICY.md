# GIOP V3.1 — Quality Policy

Status: CANONICAL

## Purpose

Define the minimum quality expected before GIOP material is treated as dependable within its declared role.

## Quality dimensions

Identity, responsibility assignment, terminology, structure, provenance, evidence, technical correctness, relation integrity, lifecycle state, accessibility, and preservation.

## Quality gates

A material artifact should pass: identity check → responsibility check → structural check → provenance check → claim/formula review → relation check → duplicate-authority check → lifecycle/version check.

## Trust distinctions

Status, validation, confidence, authority, and provenance are distinct fields and must not be used as synonyms.

## Failure handling

Known uncertainty must be labeled. A failed or incomplete validation blocks stronger claims of authority but does not require destruction of the underlying material.
