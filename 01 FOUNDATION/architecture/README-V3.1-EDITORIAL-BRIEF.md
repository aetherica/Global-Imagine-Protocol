## Version boundary

The README is V3.1-specific.
Do not introduce V4 architecture, 27-root terminology, V4-specific semantic architecture, or other future-version implementation detail into the body of the V3.1 README.

The final Evolution section explains only the transition from V3.0 to V3.1. It is not a V4 roadmap.

## Vision sequence

Use this human-centred sequence:
Human Vision → Camera Vision → Machine / Computer / Robot / AI Vision → Display → Human Perception.

The physical light/sensor/formula chain may be explained as technical machinery within the relevant sections, but it must not replace this vision-oriented narrative sequence.

## Writing style

- Natural, human, narrative-driven professional prose.
- High technical precision without bureaucratic or robotic language.
- Descriptive enough to sustain curiosity.
- Avoid repetitive “V3.0 versus V3.1” explanations across sections.
- Prefer strong transitions and complete paragraphs over excessive bullet lists.
- Explain specialist terminology in context rather than assuming the reader already knows it.
- Avoid exaggerated claims that cannot be supported.
- Preserve the sense that the repository is a destination for understanding, not merely an archive of files.

## Licensing direction

The V3.0 PDF establishes the economic philosophy:
- Free Tier: CC BY 4.0; formulas for learning, research and non-commercial use.
- Public GitHub repository: open blueprint.
- Pro License: certified GIOP binaries, OCIO configs, legal warranty for commercial studios; annual per-machine subscription.
- Enterprise License: custom integration, dedicated support and GIOP-Ready certification mark for camera manufacturers, OTT streamers and major studios.

These are baseline design elements, not immutable legal text. Future licensing policy may refine, clarify, separate, or expand rights for knowledge, data, software, certified artifacts, marks and services.

Until that policy is formally settled, README licensing prose must describe the economic model without inventing legal guarantees beyond approved policy.
