## Stress Tests
### Part Of vs Participates In
Structural constitution is kept separate from temporal/process involvement.

### Observes vs Measurement
Observation is not treated as synonymous with quantitative measurement.

### Represents vs Derived From
Representational correspondence is kept separate from provenance/lineage.

### Derived From vs Causes
Provenance is not promoted to unrestricted causal semantics.

### Has Result vs Measurement Result
`has-result` links Activity/Execution to Result without globally restricting the Result to measurement output.

### Relation Concept vs Assertion
Canonical pages define reusable relation semantics; instance assertions remain outside the concept pages.

### Relation vs Validation
Semantic/logical commitments remain distinct from data validation constraints.
