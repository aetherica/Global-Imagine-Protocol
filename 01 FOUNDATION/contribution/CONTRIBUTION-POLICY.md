# GIOP V3.1 — Contribution Policy

Status: CANONICAL

## Purpose

Provide a controlled route for adding knowledge, corrections, research, implementations, mappings, or tooling.

## Contribution requirements

A contribution must state its purpose, intended responsibility, source or provenance, proposed location, status, and expected validation path.

Contributors must not create a new responsibility root merely because an existing path is inconvenient.

## Review

Contributions that affect canonical semantics, registry identity, policy, standards, formulas, or compatibility require domain-appropriate review before canonical promotion.

## Attribution

Source attribution and contribution history must remain recoverable. Imported material retains its original provenance.

## Quality rule

A contribution is accepted because it is traceable, reviewable, and fit for its declared role, not merely because it is technically interesting.
