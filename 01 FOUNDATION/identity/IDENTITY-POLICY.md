# GIOP V3.1 — Identity Policy

Status: CANONICAL

## Purpose

Define stable identity for GIOP itself and for substantive artifacts.

## Identity principles

An artifact has an identity independent of its repository path.

A substantive record should expose a stable identifier, preferred title, artifact type, primary responsibility, lifecycle status, version, provenance, validation state, and relevant relations.

Human-readable names may change for clarity; canonical identifiers should not change merely because wording or placement changes.

## Identity boundary

FOUNDATION defines identity rules.
`02 SEMANTICS` defines meaning.
`03 REGISTRY` establishes canonical registration.

These are related but not interchangeable.
