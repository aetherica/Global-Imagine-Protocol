# GIOP V3.1 — Governance Policy

Status: CANONICAL

## Purpose

Define how authoritative GIOP decisions are made, recorded, reviewed, and changed.

## Authority model

Project authority, semantic authority, registry authority, evidence authority, implementation authority, and release authority are distinct responsibilities.

No single artifact gains semantic authority merely by being prominent, early, or widely referenced.

## Decision record

Material decisions must record the decision, rationale, affected scope, alternatives considered, evidence, and effective version.

## Review

High-impact changes require independent review appropriate to the affected domain. Disputed decisions remain traceable rather than being silently overwritten.

## Conflict rule

When documents disagree, resolve the conflict by authority, scope, version, and provenance. Do not resolve it by filename order.
