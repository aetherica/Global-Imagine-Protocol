# GIOP V3.1 — Vision

Status: CANONICAL

GIOP is intended to become durable infrastructure for imaging knowledge: understandable to a new visitor, useful to a practitioner, rigorous for a researcher, actionable for an engineer, structured for software, and interpretable by AI systems.

The long-term objective is not merely a larger archive. It is a coherent knowledge environment in which concepts, identities, formulas, evidence, implementations, standards, workflows, and historical records can be related without losing their distinctions.

The architecture should therefore remain stable enough to carry future growth while allowing the knowledge, validation state, and implementation ecosystem to evolve.
