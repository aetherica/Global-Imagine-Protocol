# GIOP V3.1 — Core Principles

Status: CANONICAL

1. One canonical body of knowledge; multiple projections and entry depths.
2. Meaning and identity are distinct responsibilities.
3. Repository location is not semantic authority.
4. Evidence, inference, implementation observation, and historical statement must remain distinguishable.
5. No silent deletion of historically relevant source material.
6. Stable identity must survive repository reorganization.
7. A new responsibility root requires a durable independent responsibility boundary.
8. Controlled terminology is infrastructure, not decoration.
9. Human readability and machine interpretability must coexist.
10. Versioning must make change understandable and traceable.
11. Normative definitions must not be silently redefined by examples or implementations.
12. Complexity should be navigable without making the knowledge less precise.
